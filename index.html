Why “Entropy”?

Because everything spreads out.

The smell of fresh coffee never stays in the cup. It drifts across the room and never gathers itself back. Physicists call that tendency entropy: there are vastly more ways for things to be spread out than to be neatly gathered, so spread out is what you get.

Curiosity works the same way. One good question never stays put, so every day we pour a new one.

How a video is brewed

From a question to an answer you can trust.

  1. 1

    Start with a real question

    The kind you already wonder about: why a mirror swaps left and right, or what really lifts a plane.

  2. 2

    Go to the source

    Research leans on primary and authoritative sources such as NASA, NIST, NOAA, CERN and peer-reviewed papers.

  3. 3

    Check every number

    Each figure is recomputed and logged in a fact-check table before the script is final. Estimates are called estimates.

  4. 4

    Explain it in motion

    Clear animation and a calm voice carry you from the everyday puzzle to the “aha!” moment.
