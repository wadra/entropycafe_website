Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Summary

This website does not ask for, collect or store personal information. Entropy Cafe Studio handles only its owner's own account data, and keeps it on the owner's own computer.

This website

  • There are no sign-ups, forms, cookies, adverts or analytics on this website.
  • The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Links lead to other services (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Amazon), which have their own privacy policies.

Entropy Cafe Studio

Entropy Cafe Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of Entropy Café to publish the channel's own videos.

  • Whose data: only the owner's own accounts are ever connected. No other person can sign in, so no other person's data is collected.
  • What it accesses on TikTok: the connected account's basic profile (such as its display name), to confirm the right account is connected, and permission to upload the owner's videos to that account.
  • What it stores: the sign-in tokens issued by each platform, kept in a local file on the owner's computer. They are not shared with anyone or sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.
  • What it does not do: it does not read other users' profiles, videos, comments or messages, and it does not sell or share any data.
  • Removing access: access can be withdrawn at any time in each platform's settings (on TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions), and the local tokens can be deleted.

Children

This website and the Entropy Café videos are intended for a general adult audience and do not knowingly collect information from children.
